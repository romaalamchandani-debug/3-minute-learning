Think Before You Send

A professional email can take 30 seconds to review—and save hours of confusion later.

Learn a simple four-part check you can use before pressing Send.

The 30-second check

Before you send, check four things.

Good emails are not about sounding formal. They are about making it easy for the reader to understand what is needed.

01 · ContextDoes the reader know why you are writing?
02 · ActionIs the required next step clear?
03 · TimelineHave you stated when it is needed?
04 · ToneDoes it sound professional and respectful?
Remember: The goal is clarity—not longer emails.
Spot the problem

This email is short. But is it clear?

Example email
Subject: Pending
To: Team
Hi,

This is still pending. Please do it ASAP as we already discussed this.

Thanks.
ContextThe reader is not told which task or issue is being referenced.
Action“Do it” does not identify the exact action.
Timeline“ASAP” leaves the deadline open to interpretation.
ToneThe wording can sound abrupt when the context is unclear.
Make it actionable

Same message. Better communication.

Before

“This is still pending. Please do it ASAP as we already discussed this.”

After

“Hi team, the Q3 tracker update is still pending. Please complete the remaining entries and share the updated file by 3:00 PM today. Thanks.”

What changed? The improved version gives the reader context, a specific action, a clear timeline, and a neutral professional tone.
