RACI in one view

Four roles. Four different expectations.

RACI works when each stakeholder understands the type of involvement expected from them.

R

Responsible

Performs the work needed to complete the task or deliverable.

A

Accountable

Owns the final outcome and has final approval or decision authority.

C

Consulted

Provides expertise or input before the work or decision is finalised.

I

Informed

Needs visibility or updates without directly contributing to the decision.

Useful principle: A task may have multiple Responsible contributors, but keeping one clear Accountable owner usually improves decision-making.
