RACI in one view

Four roles. Four different expectations.

The value of RACI is not the letters themselves. It is the clarity they create before work begins.

R

Responsible

Performs the work required to complete the task or deliverable.

A

Accountable

Owns the final result and has the authority to approve or make the final decision.

C

Consulted

Provides expertise or input before the work or decision is finalised.

I

Informed

Needs visibility or updates but does not actively contribute to the decision.

Useful rule: A task can have multiple Responsible people, but keeping one clear Accountable owner usually makes decision-making simpler.
